What makes it worth the trip

Fairy Meadows earns its name at first light, when mist burns off pine forest to reveal Nanga Parbat's sheer Rupal Face rising more than 4,000m above the campground floor. The approach is part of the experience: a hair-raising jeep track from Raikot Bridge followed by a 3–4 hour walk through forest. Wooden guesthouses and tented camps line the meadow itself, and a further two-hour hike reaches Beyal Camp, the last stop before serious mountaineers continue toward base camp proper.
