What makes it worth the trip

Concordia is the single amphitheatre where more 8,000m peaks are visible at once than anywhere else on earth, K2, Broad Peak, and the Gasherbrums ring a glacier confluence that trekkers reach after a week of walking up the Baltoro. This is a full expedition-support trek, not a casual walk: porters, cooks and permits are mandatory, camps sit on ice and moraine, and altitude gain is steady rather than steep. The reward is a K2 base camp sunrise that guides on other continents spend careers chasing.
