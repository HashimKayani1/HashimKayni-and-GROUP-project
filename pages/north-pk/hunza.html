What makes it worth the trip

Hunza sits at the meeting point of three mountain ranges, the Karakoram, Hindu Kush and Himalaya, and it shows: every viewpoint stacks glaciers behind orchards behind mud-brick forts. Attabad Lake, formed when a landslide dammed the Hunza River, now carries jet-boats and floating cafés across water so blue it looks color-graded. Base yourself in Karimabad for sunrise views of Rakaposhi and Ultar Sar, and budget a full day for the drive north to Passu's cathedral-spire peaks and cracked suspension bridges.
