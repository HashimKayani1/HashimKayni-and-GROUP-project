What makes it worth the trip

Swat pairs green-season trekking with a genuine winter ski season, a rare combination in the region. Malam Jabba's slopes and chairlift run from December through February, while summer brings alpine meadows at Kalam and Mahodand Lake, a glacial lake ringed by pine forest at 3,133m. History runs deeper than the scenery: Swat was once a center of Gandharan Buddhism, and stupa and monastery ruins near Mingora predate the region's current identity by well over a thousand years.
