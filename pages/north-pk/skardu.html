What makes it worth the trip

Skardu is where expeditions stage before disappearing into the Karakoram, but it's a destination in its own right: Shigar Fort's carved wood interiors, the cold-desert dunes at Katpana beside a glacial lake, and Upper Kachura's still, mirror-flat water. A day's drive south, the Deosai Plains open into a 4,100m alpine steppe where brown bears roam between June and September and wildflowers carpet the ground after the snow clears.
