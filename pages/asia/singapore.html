What makes it worth the trip

Singapore packs a disproportionate amount into a small footprint: Gardens by the Bay's Supertree Grove runs a nightly light show visible from across Marina Bay, the Merlion Park waterfront ties the whole skyline together in one photo, and Sentosa Island, connected by cable car or a short causeway, holds beaches, Universal Studios and an aquarium without ever feeling far from downtown. Hawker centers like Maxwell Food Centre keep the food scene affordable even as the skyline reads five-star.
