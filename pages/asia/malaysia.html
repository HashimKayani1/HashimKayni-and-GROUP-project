The Petronas Towers' skyline by night, then a duty-free archipelago strung with rainforest cable cars.

What makes it worth the trip

Kuala Lumpur's skyline is genuinely one of Asia's best after dark, and it's paired with a food scene, Jalan Alor's stalls, the multi-ethnic hawker culture across Chinatown and Little India, that easily fills a stand-alone few days. Langkawi, an hour's flight northwest, is duty-free and mostly undeveloped outside its resort strip: the SkyCab cable car climbs to a curved sky bridge over rainforest canopy, and Kilim Geoforest Park's mangrove tours run past sea eagles and limestone karsts.
