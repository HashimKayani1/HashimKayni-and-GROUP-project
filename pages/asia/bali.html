What makes it worth the trip

Bali's interior still centers on Ubud's rice terraces and temple ceremonies even as the coast has grown into a full resort economy, Tegalalang's stepped paddies and the Tirta Empul water temple both sit within a short drive of each other. Nusa Penida, a 45-minute boat ride offshore, is the harder-edged counterpart: Kelingking Beach's T-rex-shaped cliff and Angel's Billabong's tidal pools require rougher roads and earlier starts, and reward both.
