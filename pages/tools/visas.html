Before you fly

Worldwide Visa Guidelines & Checklist

Requirements vary by nationality and change without much notice, treat this as a starting checklist, then confirm against your passport's specific requirements before booking.

By region

Entry requirements at a glance

Northern Pakistan

e-Visa required for most nationalities; apply online in advance. Trekking permits needed for K2 Base Camp and Deosai.

Middle East (UAE, Qatar)

Visa-on-arrival or e-visa for most nationalities. Saudi Arabia (AlUla) offers e-visas; Oman requires an e-visa or visa-on-arrival.

Europe (Schengen)

Schengen visa covers Italy, France and Switzerland for many nationalities; Turkey requires a separate e-visa.

Southeast Asia

Thailand, Malaysia and Singapore offer visa-free entry for many nationalities; Vietnam requires an e-visa in advance.

Universal checklist

  • Passport valid 6+ months beyond travel dates
  • Two blank visa pages minimum
  • Proof of onward/return travel
  • Accommodation confirmation for the full stay
  • Travel insurance meeting destination minimums
  • Yellow fever certificate if arriving from an affected country
