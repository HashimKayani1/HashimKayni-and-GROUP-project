What makes it worth the trip

Rome rewards a slow pace more than almost any capital, the Colosseum and Forum sit a ten-minute walk from the Pantheon, and Trastevere's evenings run late without ever feeling touristy. South of Naples, the Amalfi Coast's SS163 road is the itinerary's centerpiece: switchbacks link Positano's stacked pastel houses, Amalfi's cathedral square and Ravello's clifftop gardens, with the Tyrrhenian Sea visible from nearly every kilometer.
