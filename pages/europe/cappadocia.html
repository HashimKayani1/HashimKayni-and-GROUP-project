What makes it worth the trip

Istanbul earns its own multi-day stay, the Hagia Sophia and Blue Mosque face each other across a square, the Grand Bazaar's 4,000 shops still feel navigable only by wandering, and a Bosphorus ferry crossing puts Europe and Asia on either rail of the same boat. Cappadocia, a short flight inland, trades minarets for volcanic rock: hundreds of hot-air balloons lift at dawn over Göreme's cave churches and 'fairy chimney' rock formations, one of the most photographed sunrises in travel.
