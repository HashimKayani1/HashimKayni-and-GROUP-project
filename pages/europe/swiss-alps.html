What makes it worth the trip

Zermatt bans combustion cars, which is most of why it stays so quiet given how famous its view is, the Matterhorn's near-perfect pyramid dominates the valley from almost every street. The Glacier Express connects it to St. Moritz across nearly eight hours of panoramic-window rail, crossing 91 tunnels and 291 bridges including the Landwasser Viaduct, without a single road ever intruding on the view.
