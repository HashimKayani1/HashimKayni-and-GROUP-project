What makes it worth the trip

Barcelona is Gaudí at every scale, the Sagrada Família's still-unfinished towers, Park Güell's tiled terraces, and Casa Batlló's dragon-scale roof all sit within a compact Eixample-and-Gothic-Quarter core. A 2.5-hour AVE train south, Madrid answers with the Prado's Velázquez and Goya collections, tapas crawls through La Latina, and Retiro Park's boating lake, a deliberately different register from the coast.
