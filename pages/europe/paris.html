What makes it worth the trip

Paris still sets the template it invented, the Louvre and Musée d'Orsay anchor a museum day, Montmartre's stairs and studios reward getting lost, and a Seine dinner cruise puts the Eiffel Tower's hourly light show at eye level. A TGV south to Nice trades boulevards for the Promenade des Anglais, with Èze's clifftop village and Monaco's harbor both feasible as day trips along the same stretch of coast.
