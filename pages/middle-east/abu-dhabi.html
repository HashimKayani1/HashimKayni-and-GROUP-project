What makes it worth the trip

Abu Dhabi plays a slower, grander register than Dubai. Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque holds one of the world's largest hand-knotted carpets and enough white marble to feel genuinely monumental at any hour, while Saadiyat Island's Louvre Abu Dhabi houses a collection spanning civilizations under a latticed 'rain of light' dome. Yas Island balances the culture with speed, the Yas Marina Circuit and Ferrari World sit a short drive from both.
