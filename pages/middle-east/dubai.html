What makes it worth the trip

Dubai compresses a century of ambition into a couple of decades: the Burj Khalifa still tops every skyline shot, the Dubai Mall's aquarium sits beneath a ski slope, and the old Al Fahidi quarter's wind-tower architecture is a 15-minute drive from either. Just as central to the itinerary is the desert itself, a sunset safari into the Al Marmoom or Lahbab dunes swaps skyscrapers for dune bashing, camel rides and a Bedouin-style dinner under genuinely dark skies.
