What makes it worth the trip

Musandam, cut off from the rest of Oman by UAE territory, is known locally as the 'Norway of Arabia', sheer cliffs drop straight into the Strait of Hormuz, and the standard way to see them is a full-day dhow cruise out of Khasab, with dolphin pods a near-certainty and snorkeling stops along the way. Down the coast, Muscat keeps a lower profile than its Gulf neighbors: whitewashed low-rise buildings, the Sultan Qaboos Grand Mosque, and Mutrah Souq's incense-scented lanes along a working harbor.
